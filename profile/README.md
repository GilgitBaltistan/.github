# 🏔️ Gilgit Baltistan Developer Community

> *From the roof of the world, we build.*

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for developers from **Gilgit Baltistan, Pakistan** — home to K2, the Karakoram, and now, a growing tech community.

---

## 🌍 Who We Are

We are developers, students, designers, and tech enthusiasts from the mountains of Gilgit Baltistan — Gilgit, Hunza, Skardu, Ghizer, Astore, Diamer, Ghanche, and everywhere in between.

This organization exists to:

- **Connect** GB developers scattered across Pakistan and the world
- **Collaborate** on open source projects that matter to our region
- **Inspire** the next generation of tech talent from GB
- **Represent** our region in the global tech community

---

## 🤝 How to Join

Are you a developer from Gilgit Baltistan? We want you here.

1. Open an [Issue](../../issues/new) with the title `Join Request - Your Name`
2. Tell us where you're from and what you work with
3. We'll add you to the org

No experience level required — students, beginners, and seniors are all welcome.

---

## 💻 What We Work On

- Open source tools built for and by GB communities
- Student projects from KIU, UOBS and other institutions
- Local problem solving (connectivity, tourism, agriculture tech)
- Anything our members are passionate about

---

## 🗺️ Our Region

Gilgit Baltistan is home to:
- **5 of the world's 14 eight-thousanders** including K2, the world's second highest peak
- **Karakoram International University (KIU)** — the region's top university
- A young, ambitious population ready to make its mark in tech
